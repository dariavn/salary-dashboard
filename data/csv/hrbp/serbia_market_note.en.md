Public salary data for HR roles in Serbia is very limited — most local employers don't disclose pay bands on job boards, and salary aggregators (Glassdoor, Levels.fyi, Payscale) have only a handful of often unrepresentative submissions for Serbia.

The one strong anchor across the entire dataset is **Mozzart Bet**, Serbia's largest iGaming operator: HR Manager ~$102K/yr (Glassdoor, n=39). It underpins the Head/premium/iGaming estimate. Direct Fintech data is nearly absent — the Middle-segment figure shown is loosely carried over from an unrelated company (Yandex) as a rough "premium employer" proxy, and doesn't reflect fintech specifically.

Junior/Middle estimates are mostly general-market (not IT-specific): HR Business Partner rarely exists as a standalone junior title in Serbia — the role is typically hired starting from Middle level. Separately confirmed: the title "HR Project Manager" doesn't appear on local job boards at all — that position was folded into HRBP.
